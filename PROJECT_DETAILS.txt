1. THE BIG PICTURE: HOW IT WORKS (FOR NON-TECHIES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Imagine you have a 1,000-page book and you want to ask a question about a 
specific character. You wouldn't read the whole book to the AI every time—that 
would be slow and expensive!

Instead, we use a concept called RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation):
1. THE SEARCH: We chop the video transcript into small "note cards."
2. THE MAP: we turn those cards into numbers (Vectors) and store them in a 
   digital "map" (Pinecone).
3. THE LOOKUP: When you ask a question, we find the 5 closest "note cards" on 
   that map.
4. THE ANSWER: We show only those 5 cards to GPT-4o, and it gives you a perfect 
   answer instantly.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE CLOUD TECHNOLOGY STACK (THE "POWERHOUSE")
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We have upgraded this project from a local tool to a cloud-native application:

☁️ SUPABASE (The Permanent Memory)
- Role: This is our main database.
- Layman terms: Think of this as the project's "File Cabinet." It stores the 
  video title, the full transcript, and the summary. Because it's in the cloud, 
  your data never gets lost even if you turn off your computer.
- Tech: PostgreSQL (The world's most powerful database).

🌲 PINECONE (The Digital Brain Map)
- Role: This is our "Vector Database."
- Layman terms: This is a specialized warehouse that only stores coordinates. 
  It knows exactly where every sentence of a video "lives" in a 3D space 
  based on its meaning.
- Tech: Serverless Vector Indexing.

🤖 OPENAI (The Intelligence)
- GPT-4o: The "Genius Guest" that reads the transcript and writes human-like 
  summaries and answers.
- text-embedding-3-small: The "Translator" that turns human words into the 
  numbers (math) that Pinecone understands.

🐍 FASTAPI & PYTHON 3.14 (The Engine)
- Role: The "Manager" that handles everything. It talks to YouTube, sends data 
  to OpenAI, and fetches results from Supabase.
- Python 3.14: We are using the cutting-edge, newest version of Python for 
  maximum speed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE FILE INTERACTION: WHO TALKS TO WHOM?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Here is the journey of a YouTube Link through our code:

1. [ USER ] enters a Link → Handed to 'static/app.js'
2. 'static/app.js' → Sends a request to 'main.py' (/analyze)
3. 'main.py' → Asks 'services/youtube.py' for the transcript.
4. 'services/youtube.py' → Scrapes YouTube (using yt-dlp if needed).
5. 'main.py' → Asks 'services/openai_service.py' to summarize it.
6. 'main.py' → Asks 'vector_db.py' to "Map the text" into Pinecone.
7. 'main.py' → Saves the final result in Supabase via 'database.py'.
8. [ USER ] asks a question → 'main.py' (/chat) finds the answer in Pinecone 
   and GPT-4o, then sends it back to the screen!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. FILE-BY-FILE BREAKDOWN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- main.py: The "Traffic Controller." It connects the frontend to the backend.
- database.py: The "Secretary." It manages the connection to Supabase.
- vector_db.py: The "Navigator." It manages the connection to Pinecone.
- services/youtube.py: The "Scout." It finds and extracts subtitles from hidden 
  parts of YouTube.
- services/openai_service.py: The "Writer." It creates summaries and answers.
- static/index.html & style.css: The "Skin." This is the beautiful glassmorphic 
  UI you see on your screen.
